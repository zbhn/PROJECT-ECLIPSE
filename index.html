🔬 NASA SCIENTIFIC & AEROSPACE BREAKTHROUGHS

⚡
2.0° SKIP REENTRY PROFILE

Unlike Apollo's direct 6.5° plunge, Orion entered at Mach 32 and skipped out of the upper exosphere to shed 2,000 m/s kinetic energy, lowering peak deceleration from 12 Gs to 4 Gs for crew safety.

🛰️
GATEWAY NRHO ORBIT

Near-Rectilinear Halo Orbit balances Earth-Moon gravity, requiring minimal station-keeping Delta-V (~2 m/s per year) while offering unbroken communication with Earth and the lunar South Pole.

🧊
SHACKLETON WATER-ICE

Permanent shadows inside Shackleton Crater preserve ancient water-ice deposits at -246°C, providing the bedrock resource for lunar base life support and rocket propellant on the path to Mars.

🚀
SLS BLOCK 1B CO-MANIFEST

The powerful Exploration Upper Stage (EUS) boosted 38 metric tons to Trans-Lunar Injection, delivering Orion plus the 10-ton European I-Hab module in a single launch.
